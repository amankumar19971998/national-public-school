About Us

Building Tomorrow's Leaders

Since 1985, National Public School has been at the forefront of educational excellence, nurturing thousands of students who have gone on to become leaders in various fields. Our commitment to holistic development ensures that every child receives the best possible foundation for their future.

With state-of-the-art infrastructure, experienced faculty, and innovative teaching methodologies, we create an environment where students thrive academically, socially, and emotionally.
